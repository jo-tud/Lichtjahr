Open-source family photo calendar

Your family’s year, on the wall.

Sort a year of photos into months, lay out the pages, and get a color-managed CMYK PDF that is ready for the print shop. Lichtjahr does the fiddly parts for you – and shows you the finished calendar hanging on the wall before you order it.

Try it in your browser Download version 1.3
  • Free and open source (MIT)
  • macOS, Windows, Linux
  • English and German

See it on the wall before you print it

The calendar hangs on a wall and its pages turn over the wire binding – with a click, a swipe or the arrow keys. Switch on the print preview and the photos look the way the press will put them on paper.

Less fiddling, more of your photos

Lichtjahr takes care of the things that usually take an evening each year.

Photos sorted by month

From folder names, capture dates or file names. Drop photos into the month folders whenever you like; the app keeps up.

Enhance photos

Automatic exposure and white balance that treat every skin tone alike, backlight and noise fixed locally, looks, and a print loupe that shows a spot as it will be printed.

Match all months

Brings exposure and color mood of the twelve months closer together, so the calendar looks all of a piece – the seasons stay.

Faces kept in view

Automatic crops keep everyone in the picture, and the check list flags faces cut off at the edge.

Who appears how often

Counts who is in how many photos and how often in the calendar, so nobody in the family is left out. Entirely on your computer.

Burst series

Five nearly identical shots? Lichtjahr suggests the sharpest, well-exposed one and sets the rest aside with one click.

Birthdays and holidays

Public holidays by German state, school holidays as a fine line, week numbers and birthdays, also from your contacts.

In your Nextcloud

The project folder can live in your own Nextcloud; the app password stays on your computer only.

iPhone photos

Reads HEIC photos and keeps the wider Display P3 colors all the way to print.

The calendar grid finds its own spot

It looks for a calm area in the photo – sky, meadow, water – picks a legible text color and never covers a face. Four designs, equal-width figures and clear week breaks keep every month tidy.

Made for the print shop

With the local service, Lichtjahr writes the PDF the print shop asks for – with your print shop’s color profile and a check report.

  • PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 with the print shop’s ICC profile, converted with LittleCMS
  • Bundled profiles for coated and uncoated paper, or add your print shop’s own
  • Preflight on the finished file: CMYK only, ink coverage, bleed, fonts, resolution
  • On-screen soft proof with paper white and a hatching where colors won’t print
  • Photos adapted to paper: lighter midtones, opened shadows, output sharpening

Checked with Lichtjahr’s own preflight, not a certified tool. Format, page count and binding come from your print shop.

Your photos stay yours

No account, no cloud service of ours, no upload. Lichtjahr works with a folder on your computer – or in your own Nextcloud.

Your folder

Photos stay in plain month folders. Originals are never changed.

Your computer

The service runs locally; the online version keeps everything in your browser.

Faces stay local

Face features live only in the local cache. The project only stores names.

Your passwords

Nextcloud app passwords are stored on your computer only, never in the project.

On the sofa, too

The app works on the phone: pick photos, adjust a crop, flip through the wall. One window edits at a time; the others follow along.

Get started

Free, no account, no sign-up.

Just try it

The online version starts with sample pictures. Your own photos stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Open the online version

With your photo folder

  1. Download Lichtjahr-1.2.1.zip and unpack it.
  2. Double-click Lichtjahr.command (macOS) or Lichtjahr.bat (Windows); on Linux run ./lichtjahr.sh.
  3. Lichtjahr opens in your browser. The first start sets everything up in your user folder (about 150 MB).
