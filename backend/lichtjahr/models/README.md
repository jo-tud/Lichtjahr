# Models

`face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx` – YuNet face detector from the
[OpenCV Zoo](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet),
by Shiqi Yu and contributors, MIT license (see `LICENSE-yunet.txt`). It finds faces and their position;
it does not recognize who someone is. Runs with OpenCV (`cv2.FaceDetectorYN`), on the CPU, entirely locally.
